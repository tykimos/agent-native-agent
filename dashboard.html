What should this ANA do?

Tell ANA in the chat — what the work is, who it is for, what you want to see. It asks what it needs, writes this PRD and builds the first screens with you. Drawing helps too: sketch a screen roughly and send it.

What is a PRD? A one-page description of the product you want: what it is for, who uses it, and what it does. ANA builds the screens from it, and keeps it up to date as the app changes.
ExampleStudio sales — for me and my partner · we track leads → quotes → follow-ups · I want a list of who to call this week, and a reminder when a quote gets no answer for 3 days.
Step 1 · Shape the PRD together. Press Talk to ANA and ANA starts the conversation — it asks what product you want to make, then one short question at a time. The PRD is written as you go; nothing is built until you agree.
