# My ANA

> This is a sample. Change it to say what **your** ANA is for — edit it here, or tell the agent in chat and it will update this page. Then press *Build Work from the PRD* on the Work tab.

## Purpose
What this ANA helps with, and for whom. *(e.g. "Run the sales pipeline of a two-person studio: leads, quotes, follow-ups.")*

## Users
Who uses it and what each of them needs. *(e.g. "Me — sees what to do today. My partner — approves quotes.")*

## Work
What the work is made of and how it flows — the things people handle, what they need to see and do. The Work tab is built from this section.
- *(e.g. "Leads: name, company, source, stage. A lead becomes a quote; a quote becomes a project.")*
- *(e.g. "A weekly view of follow-ups that are due.")*

## Knowledge
What is worth remembering as the work goes on — the kinds of things and how they relate. *(e.g. "Clients, the people at each client, what they bought.")* The Knowledge tab starts empty and grows from the work.

## Agent
What the agent does on its own, and what it should ask first. *(e.g. "Drafts follow-up emails, but never sends without my OK.")*

## What is here now
- **PRD** — this page. Every save is a new version; History keeps the earlier ones.
- **Work** — empty until it is built from the PRD.
- **Knowledge** — Ontology (kinds and relations) and Graph (things and facts), empty at first.
- **Requests** — what the agent needs from you: approvals, decisions, access, things only you can do.
- **Evolve** — proposals for what to do now and how this ANA could change.
- **Update** — new features from agent-native-agent, brought in by the agent.
- **Chat** — talk to the coding agent that runs this ANA; it changes the app as you talk.

## Not yet
Known gaps and ideas for later.
